Your season. Your role. Your card. A position-aware player rating, earned progression, and collectible editions built around verified hockey performance.

CHL SEASON 55 · RATING BETA
01 · PERFORMANCE

Elite Chel Overall (ECO)

An experimental 60–99 card rating, with a separate 0–100 performance index built from season statistics within each role. Centers, wingers, defensemen and goalies are evaluated with different weights. ECO is not a measure of how many games you grind.

02 · PROGRESSION

Card XP & editions

Earn XP from the same consistently available official game statistics for all eligible players. XP advances your card level and visual tier. Your ECO rating stays separate, so grinding games alone cannot create a 99-overall player.

HOW THE RATING WORKS

Different jobs. Different weights.

Select a position to see the ECM beta formula. Each position totals exactly 100%. Weights are ECM design choices, not a validated measure of hockey ability. Eligible players receive a role-relative beta score in the card builder.

TOTAL: 100%

CHL Season 55 regular season only. Minimum 6 games and 20 eligible peers in your role; goalies also need 100 shots faced. Per-game rates are adjusted toward the role median with a 6-game prior; passing uses 200 attempts and save percentage uses 100 shots. Tied percentiles use midrank. A performance index of 50 maps to an ECM card OVR of 80. The display mapping is round(60 + 0.39 × index); it is a product scale, not a validated EA-equivalent skill rating. Missing required metrics leave OVR pending. GAA also depends on team defense.

How you earn progression

01 · Play

Compete in recorded league games. Only eligible, verified results count.

02 · Perform

Earn role-relevant XP from consistently recorded official game statistics. Optional VOD insights do not affect league-wide XP.

03 · Upgrade

Unlock higher card levels, visual tiers and achievement editions. ECO is calculated independently.

Fair-play rule: XP awards, thresholds and anti-farming limits are still being tested. Only consistently available statistics count toward league-wide XP. VOD events are optional scouting context, not extra progression points. Plus/minus is contextual and is not proof of individual defensive skill.

SPECIAL EDITIONS

Signature & achievement cards

Earn limited visual editions for verified milestones: Signature, Breakout, Hot Streak and Defensive Wall. These editions celebrate achievement; they do not automatically inflate ECO.

Custom player images and signatures will require player permission before publication.

Qualifying teams can receive postseason-themed cards. Proposed editions include Playoff Debut, Series Standout and Clutch Performer, based on verified playoff results.

No playoff cards are awarded until qualification, eligibility and achievements are confirmed.
