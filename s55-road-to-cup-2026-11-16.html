Road to the Cup: Nobody Has Advanced Yet

The WHL, OHL and QMJHL wildcard paths after Monday's verified finals.

Playoff Desk · Monday, November 16, 2026 · Updated after the November 16 finals

Night in ReviewPressure CookerRivalry ReportRoad to the CupAll 12 Series

Six teams have three wins. Nobody has four. The race to the Memorial Cup is heating up, but the only responsible bracket update is the one supported by confirmed game results.

Evidence standard: Confirmed team scores and series standings from the EliteChel Media LeagueGaming LGCHL parser, refreshed November 17, 2026, 1:17 AM ET. Individual November 16 player lines, goal scorers, shots and saves are not yet available in the league-wide box-score import. Player contributions are not inferred. Four other scheduled QMJHL games involving Halifax, Charlottetown, Cape Breton and Chicoutimi remain unreported and are excluded from the 24-final total.

WHL: two deadlocks, two three-win leaders

Chilliwack Bruins vs. Edmonton Oil Kings · Series tied 2–2

The key is how both sides respond after close finishes. Neither team managed two consecutive wins through four games. Chilliwack has produced 15 goals across the series, while Edmonton has 15 as well. That perfect balance makes the fifth game a fresh pressure test, not an automatic advantage for either seed. Four-game ledger.

Swift Current Broncos vs. Red Deer Rebels · Series tied 2–2

The contrast is impossible to ignore: Red Deer scored 13 in Sunday's pair and just one across Monday's two finals. Swift Current did more than answer on offense; it reduced the Rebels' scoring to almost nothing in Game 4. That is a team-level defensive story, not proof that any individual defender or goalie delivered a specific play. Four-game ledger.

Kelowna Rockets vs. Tri City Americans · Tri City Americans leads 3–1

The striking detail is the back-to-back score reversal. Kelowna showed it can drive six past Tri City, but the Americans immediately proved that result was no new normal. With six goals of their own in the second Monday game, Tri City prevented the series from sliding into a 2–2 toss-up. Four-game ledger.

Victoria Royals vs. Penticton Vees · Penticton Vees leads 3–1

The Royals' 5–1 win is more than a consolation score: it interrupted the sweep and forced another night of hockey. Still, Penticton holds the significant advantage at 3–1 and needs only one more win to claim a best-of-seven matchup. Victoria must keep repeating its last result, without a single off night. Four-game ledger.

OHL: three ties and a North Bay advantage

Kingston Frontenacs vs. North Bay Battalion · North Bay Battalion leads 3–1

The Battalion and Frontenacs split Sunday's games. North Bay won 6–1, while Kingston answered 5–3. Monday was a very different night: North Bay won 5–3 and 3–2 on Kingston's home side of the schedule to build a 3–1 series advantage. Four-game ledger.

Brampton Steelheads vs. Sudbury Wolves · Series tied 2–2

Brampton's 2–1 win and Sudbury's 4–2 response split Sunday. Monday followed the same script: the Steelheads won 3–2, then the Wolves answered 2–1. Through four games, neither team has taken two straight, and the series stands 2–2. Four-game ledger.

Erie Otters vs. Sarnia Sting · Series tied 2–2

Erie won the opening game 4–1 before Sarnia took the second 6–5. Monday repeated the split: the Otters posted a 5–1 victory, only for the Sting to answer 5–3 in Game 4. It is 2–2 after four, with the pressure completely reset. Four-game ledger.

Flint Firebirds vs. Saginaw Spirit · Series tied 2–2

Saginaw opened with a pair of narrow wins, 3–2 and 2–1, to take control of Sunday. Flint answered with its own two-game sweep on Monday, winning 2–1 in overtime and 3–1 in regulation. From 0–2 to 2–2, the Firebirds have brought the series back to even. Four-game ledger.

QMJHL: three leaders, Shawinigan's turnaround

Charlottetown Islanders vs. Cape Breton Eagles · Cape Breton Eagles leads 3–1

Cape Breton's first two games were 3–0 and 2–0 wins, a spotless start that gave Charlottetown nothing on the scoreboard. The Islanders finally broke through on Monday with a 4–1 win. The Eagles responded by winning Game 4 by a 4–3 margin and now lead the series 3–1. Four-game ledger.

Quebec Remparts vs. Chicoutimi Sagueneens · Chicoutimi Sagueneens leads 3–1

Chicoutimi won the first two games, 3–2 in overtime and 4–3. The Saguenéens then added a 6–3 victory to open Monday and move within one win of advancing. Quebec refused to let the series end there, winning Game 4 by a 4–1 score. Four-game ledger.

Shawinigan Cataractes vs. Victoriaville Tigres · Series tied 2–2

Victoriaville controlled Sunday, winning 3–2 and 6–3 for a quick 2–0 lead. On Monday, Shawinigan took both games at home: 4–1 followed by 2–1. The Cataractes have erased the entire deficit and the series is now locked at 2–2. Four-game ledger.

Blainville-Boisbriand Armada vs. Val-d'Or Foreurs · Val-d'Or Foreurs leads 3–1

Blainville-Boisbriand opened with a 5–1 victory, but Val-d'Or came back in Sunday's second game, winning 6–5 in overtime. On Monday the Foreurs took both games, 7–5 and 6–5 in overtime, to seize a 3–1 series advantage. Four-game ledger.
