Three moments that changed the bracket

Swift Current makes Red Deer's 2–0 lead disappear

Sunday's 10-goal Red Deer statement is now history. Swift Current won both Monday games 4–1 and 1–0, turning a 0–2 start into a 2–2 series. The Broncos' Game 4 shutout is the clearest defensive turnaround in the reported scores.

Flint wins twice against Saginaw

Two narrow Saginaw wins began this rivalry; Flint reversed it with Monday victories of 2–1 in overtime and 3–1. Four games in, they are even. The difference between an early series advantage and an actual series win has never been clearer.

Val-d'Or takes over the shootout with Blainville-Boisbriand

The Foreurs scored 7 and 6 in Monday's two victories and now lead 3–1. The second result required overtime; the matchup remains dangerous even to the team holding three wins.
