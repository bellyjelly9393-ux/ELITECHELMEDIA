Two games into the wildcards, some clubs looked untouchable. Four games in, the bracket is full of answers and counters. The most revealing rivalries are the ones that refused to stay settled.

Deadlocked matchups: six series at 2–2

The defining twist is how quickly early leads vanished. Both Red Deer and Saginaw began 2–0; Swift Current and Flint won both Monday games to force a fresh contest.

Chilliwack Bruins vs. Edmonton Oil Kings

Chilliwack and Edmonton have reached a genuine stalemate. The Bruins took Sunday's opener 7–5 before the Oil Kings answered 3–1. On Monday, the teams exchanged identical 4–3 victories, each protecting its home-side opportunity in the box score. The series is tied 2–2, with no cushion left for either club. Full rivalry report.

Swift Current Broncos vs. Red Deer Rebels

Red Deer opened with a 10–5 eruption and a 3–1 follow-up, taking a two-game lead and looking capable of overwhelming Swift Current. Monday rewrote that storyline. The Broncos won 4–1, then blanked the Rebels 1–0. A two-game deficit is gone. The series is 2–2. Full rivalry report.

Brampton Steelheads vs. Sudbury Wolves

Brampton's 2–1 win and Sudbury's 4–2 response split Sunday. Monday followed the same script: the Steelheads won 3–2, then the Wolves answered 2–1. Through four games, neither team has taken two straight, and the series stands 2–2. Full rivalry report.

Erie Otters vs. Sarnia Sting

Erie won the opening game 4–1 before Sarnia took the second 6–5. Monday repeated the split: the Otters posted a 5–1 victory, only for the Sting to answer 5–3 in Game 4. It is 2–2 after four, with the pressure completely reset. Full rivalry report.

Flint Firebirds vs. Saginaw Spirit

Saginaw opened with a pair of narrow wins, 3–2 and 2–1, to take control of Sunday. Flint answered with its own two-game sweep on Monday, winning 2–1 in overtime and 3–1 in regulation. From 0–2 to 2–2, the Firebirds have brought the series back to even. Full rivalry report.

Shawinigan Cataractes vs. Victoriaville Tigres

Victoriaville controlled Sunday, winning 3–2 and 6–3 for a quick 2–0 lead. On Monday, Shawinigan took both games at home: 4–1 followed by 2–1. The Cataractes have erased the entire deficit and the series is now locked at 2–2. Full rivalry report.
