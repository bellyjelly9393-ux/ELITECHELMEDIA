HOW THE CONTEST WILL WORK

PREDICTOR RULES · PROPOSED

Round 1 late-entry community picks reopen through November 16 at 9:30 p.m. ET. These picks do not earn Round 1 points or grand-prize eligibility; regular scoring resumes for new entrants in Round 2. Entry is free. Prize details are pending.

  1. Submit a winner and series length (4–7 games) for every matchup in the selected round.
  2. Original Round 1 ballots locked Sunday, November 15 at 9:00 p.m. ET. New participants can enter late community picks until Monday, November 16 at 9:30 p.m. ET, before the next scheduled games. Original ballots cannot be edited.
  3. One account per person. Late Round 1 picks are community-only: no Round 1 points or grand-prize eligibility. Everyone can earn normal points from Round 2 onward. Only officially confirmed results award points.
  4. A one-time share of an official EliteChel Media playoff video in a community that permits promotion is planned. Exact proof and eligibility requirements will be announced after the contest review. Never spam a server.
  5. No purchase is required. Final prizes, entry dates, eligibility, tiebreakers and other legally required terms will be published before entries open.

Rules above are a preview, not the final binding contest terms. EliteChel Media is independent fan coverage. LeagueGaming retains its own official results and rules.
