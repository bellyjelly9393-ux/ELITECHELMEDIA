The Pressure Cooker: One Win From the Edge

Six clubs lead 3–1, six showdowns are tied. The next playoff game changes everything.

Playoff Desk · Monday, November 16, 2026 · Updated after the November 16 finals

There is no four-win series clinch yet. But half the matchups now have a team one victory from closing. The other half have no advantage at all.

One win away: the six 3–1 leaders

These clubs need one more victory to secure their best-of-seven series. Their opponents need to win three in a row. That is a mathematical reality, not an editorial prediction.

Tri City Americans leads Kelowna Rockets, 3–1

Tri City swept its first two games 5–2 and 5–3, putting the Rockets on the edge early. Kelowna finally struck with a 6–1 victory on Monday, a decisive answer that made this matchup look different. The Americans responded with their own 6–1 final, restoring a 3–1 series lead. See all four finals.

Penticton Vees leads Victoria Royals, 3–1

Penticton took both Sunday games, 4–1 and 3–2. The Vees then found a 2–1 overtime victory in Monday's first meeting to push the series to 3–0. That was the point at which a sweep became a real possibility. Victoria responded by winning the next game 5–1. See all four finals.

North Bay Battalion leads Kingston Frontenacs, 3–1

The Battalion and Frontenacs split Sunday's games. North Bay won 6–1, while Kingston answered 5–3. Monday was a very different night: North Bay won 5–3 and 3–2 on Kingston's home side of the schedule to build a 3–1 series advantage. See all four finals.

Cape Breton Eagles leads Charlottetown Islanders, 3–1

Cape Breton's first two games were 3–0 and 2–0 wins, a spotless start that gave Charlottetown nothing on the scoreboard. The Islanders finally broke through on Monday with a 4–1 win. The Eagles responded by winning Game 4 by a 4–3 margin and now lead the series 3–1. See all four finals.

Chicoutimi Sagueneens leads Quebec Remparts, 3–1

Chicoutimi won the first two games, 3–2 in overtime and 4–3. The Saguenéens then added a 6–3 victory to open Monday and move within one win of advancing. Quebec refused to let the series end there, winning Game 4 by a 4–1 score. See all four finals.

Val-d'Or Foreurs leads Blainville-Boisbriand Armada, 3–1

Blainville-Boisbriand opened with a 5–1 victory, but Val-d'Or came back in Sunday's second game, winning 6–5 in overtime. On Monday the Foreurs took both games, 7–5 and 6–5 in overtime, to seize a 3–1 series advantage. See all four finals.

The equal-pressure six: tied 2–2

Six other series are still square. A Game 5 loss is not elimination, but it will put the losing side one defeat from the end.

Chilliwack Bruins vs. Edmonton Oil Kings · 2–2

The key is how both sides respond after close finishes. Neither team managed two consecutive wins through four games. Chilliwack has produced 15 goals across the series, while Edmonton has 15 as well. That perfect balance makes the fifth game a fresh pressure test, not an automatic advantage for either seed. Game-by-game results.

Swift Current Broncos vs. Red Deer Rebels · 2–2

The contrast is impossible to ignore: Red Deer scored 13 in Sunday's pair and just one across Monday's two finals. Swift Current did more than answer on offense; it reduced the Rebels' scoring to almost nothing in Game 4. That is a team-level defensive story, not proof that any individual defender or goalie delivered a specific play. Game-by-game results.

Brampton Steelheads vs. Sudbury Wolves · 2–2

Monday's margins were both one goal, keeping pressure on each shift and each defensive decision. Sudbury ended the evening with the latest win, but Brampton proved earlier in the night that it can take a close result in the same matchup. Nobody owns a numerical series advantage. Game-by-game results.

Erie Otters vs. Sarnia Sting · 2–2

The same night showed two versions of this matchup. Erie can keep Sarnia to one goal when it gets its preferred result, but the Sting have already demonstrated two separate five-plus-goal responses. The combined four-game ledger is close enough that Game 5 becomes a real pivot, not a ceremony. Game-by-game results.

Flint Firebirds vs. Saginaw Spirit · 2–2

The pattern is especially sharp: the first three games were one-goal results, then Flint created a two-goal margin in Game 4. Saginaw now has to solve the same opponent that it beat twice less than a day earlier. Flint has momentum in results, but the series score gives neither team a cushion. Game-by-game results.

Shawinigan Cataractes vs. Victoriaville Tigres · 2–2

Shawinigan's defensive improvement is visible in the team scores: the Tigres went from nine goals Sunday to two Monday. The Cataractes combined six goals of their own Monday while denying Victoriaville the chance to extend the series lead. That does not guarantee the next result, but it changes where the pressure sits. Game-by-game results.
