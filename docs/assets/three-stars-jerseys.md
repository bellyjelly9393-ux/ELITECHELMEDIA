The transparent display cutouts were prepared with image generation from these team-store references, preserving team colours, crest and striping while arranging sleeves for a narrow locker. They are illustrative display artwork, not photographs of an LG player's equipment. No player jersey number is assigned. Optimized to WebP with alpha, at most 600 × 720 pixels.

Teams without a mapped jersey retain their team crest. A failed jersey request also falls back to the team's crest. Add future jerseys by canonical LG team ID; never reuse a different team's uniform.
